Find Indian mutual funds by sector exposure

Find My Mutual Fund is a free mutual fund sector exposure screener. Instead of comparing schemes only by returns or category, use disclosed portfolio allocations to compare how much of each fund is invested across sectors. Set your own limits, apply the rules, and review the allocation breakdown behind each result.

How to screen mutual funds by sector allocation

  • Choose a sector and set a rule: exclude it, cap its allocation, set a minimum, or leave it unrestricted.
  • Apply rules for the exposures that matter to you, such as IT, banking or pharma.
  • Filter by fund category or asset management company (AMC), or search for a scheme by name.
  • Compare the rule-by-rule differences and check the disclosure month before using the result.

Screen for low IT, banking, pharma and other sector exposure

The screener supports IT, Banking, Financial Services, Pharma & Healthcare, Auto, FMCG, Energy/Oil & Gas, Power, Metals & Mining, Capital Goods, Construction/Infra, Telecom, Consumer Durables, Realty, Chemicals and Others. For example, you can set IT exposure to 0% to find schemes whose reported IT allocation meets that rule, or set a maximum for Banking or Energy/Oil & Gas.

What does the exposure-match score mean?

The score measures the percentage-point gap between a fund's disclosed sector allocations and your selected rules. A lower score means a closer match to those specific rules; it does not mean the fund is safer, will perform better, or is suitable for you. Review the full portfolio and your own investment needs separately.

Data coverage and limitations

This public beta uses monthly AMC portfolio disclosures and currently covers selected scheme-matched portfolios from SBI Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund and HDFC Mutual Fund. The current release is based on September 2026 disclosures and covers 84 schemes. Coverage is limited, sector mapping can group some source industries under "Others", and data may be delayed. Check the relevant AMC factsheet for confirmation.
